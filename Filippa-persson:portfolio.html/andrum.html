ANDRUM

A project that started in the course User Studies in Interaction Design which focuses on the need for recovery in a busy society. The project was carried out as a fictional collaboration with Skanska and the City of Stockholm. Based on Value Sensitive Design (VSD), interviews were conducted with professionals and students around Humlegården. The study showed that although many people value peace and quiet, they find it difficult to prioritize breaks in a busy everyday life filled with work or studies. Fresh air, movement and music were identified as important parts of relaxation. These insights led to the idea of creating an inviting park bench for spontaneous breaks.

The concept was then developed into a physical and digital prototype as part of the course Prototyping in Interaction Design II. The physical bench combines steel and wood with features that support accessibility and safety. The prototype also includes Arduino-controlled lighting and a SwiftUI app with weather data.

The final result was a complete design system that invites people to slow down, recharge and reconnect with their surroundings in an urban environment, based on the needs and wishes of the users.
