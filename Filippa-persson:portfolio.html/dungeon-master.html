Dungeon Master

A design created during a lab in the course Human–AI Interaction Design, focusing on designing an interface where AI acts as an active collaborator. The task was to create an AI-powered Game Master for Dungeons & Dragons, with the goal of making it easy and fun for the user to play together with the AI.

Based on research about the game and its visual style, the entire chat interface was designed from scratch. The AI was set up to respond in an exciting and engaging way. To help the player, suggestions were added to the chat field to show what they could write. It was also made easy to ask for a new response if the player was not happy with the AI’s suggestion.

The result was an interactive prototype where the player and AI create a story together in real time, through an interface that is easy to understand and control.
