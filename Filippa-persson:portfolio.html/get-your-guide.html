Rebranding

A group project where the focus was to explore the possibilities and limitations of colors and images.

Starting from a platform of our choice, we chose to redesign GetYourGuide by only updating the color palette and visual style of the images to make it feel more inspiring and personal, without changing the layout or typography. The colors were chosen to inspire people to travel while also creating a harmonious experience for the user. We combined light blue and orange, where blue contributes to a feeling of safety and trust, while orange adds creativity and encouragement according to color psychology.

For the visual style, we replaced well-edited advertising photos with more authentic and realistic images that users could have taken themselves. This makes the experience more relatable and shows what the destinations actually look like in real life.

The final result was a visual improvement that replaces stiff advertising images with real travel inspiration, where color and images work together to create curiosity and engagement for the user.
