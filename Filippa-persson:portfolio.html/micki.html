2022

Micki Glassbil

The project was carried out as a product development project in a fictional collaboration with the toy manufacturer Micki Leksaker. The starting point was Micki's long tradition of manufacturing wooden toys that encourage playful learning and creativity. The challenge was to create a new product concept that preserves the company's classic craftsmanship, while meeting today's demands for sustainable material choices, educational value and modern children. The purpose was to combine an aesthetic design with a functional and safe construction.

To reach the concept, an iterative design process was applied. The work began with a pilot study and target audience analysis to map trends and user needs. Based on these insights, a specification of requirements was established that took safety standards, material choices and manufacturing conditions into account. This was followed by idea generation and sketching where different concepts were explored. Finally, the most promising idea was modeled in the 3D-CAD tool Inventor.

The work resulted in a well-developed toy concept adapted for Micki's product range, where the final result was presented through a polystyrene foam prototype in a 1:1 scale, along with technical documentation consisting of CAD models, drawings, and material specifications. The product meets high standards for both manufacturability and safety, while offering children an engaging play experience.
