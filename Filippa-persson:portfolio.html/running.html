50K

A personal design project created to celebrate a person’s achievement of planning and completing a 50-kilometer race. The idea was to create a visual alternative to a traditional medal in the form of a clean and simple poster.

Starting from an existing template, the design was developed into a new version and adapted to the runner. The actual route profile was used as a central element of the design, giving the poster a unique and personal touch.
